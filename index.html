GAME UPDATES

News & Update Log

v1.51 — Shop & Brick Buff Fix

Shop buttons remain mounted during rapid updates so clicks register reliably. Golden and Rainbow Brick rewards now display a gold buff name with a smaller explanation underneath.

v1.50 — Autosave Repair

Fixed startup errors caused by brick overlays loading after game scripts. Saves and Golden/Rainbow Bricks now initialize in the correct order, without resetting existing progress.

v1.49 — Bricks Everywhere

Golden and Rainbow Brick overlays now appear above every tab, including shops, achievements, and settings, without interrupting spawn timers or rewards.

v1.48 — Research Lab Reliability Fix

Research buttons now stay mounted while game values update, preventing clicks from being swallowed during frequent screen refreshes.

v1.46 — The Great Rebalance

Increased normal shop prices across all tiers, with the biggest changes for cosmic and beyond-vigintillion upgrades. Repeat purchases now scale by 1.85× instead of 1.55×, making new tiers meaningful long-term goals rather than instant purchases. Existing saves and upgrade ownership remain intact.

v1.45 — Beyond Vigintillion

Expanded compact number names through centillion (Ce), uncentillion (UCe), and the maximum JavaScript number range. Added 40 new lifetime wealth achievements and 17 extreme late-game production upgrades to reach those milestones. No scientific notation in displayed numbers. The +1× per claimed ascension system stays unchanged.

v1.44 — Compact Numbers & Claimed Ascensions

All quantities use compact K, M, B and T abbreviations without scientific notation. Beyond one trillion, totals keep stacking in T. Ascensions now award an additive +1× bonus only after being claimed; unclaimed milestones do not increase production.

v1.43 — The Reset Rescue Update

Burn All Studs and save imports now survive page reloads without being overwritten by the save-on-exit handler. Added a separate Full Wipe button that deletes all game progress, permanent unlocks, challenge rewards, saved backups, and records after a typed confirmation.

v1.42 — Rainbow Rave & Cosmic Couture

Rainbow Bricks give exactly one shard and trigger one of four immense temporary powers. Rainbow Luck moved to Rainbows Rave with ten very expensive stud-cost levels. Stud Specials now includes a permanent hat catalogue with a top hat. Expanded endgame production, vigintillion lifetime achievements, and retired obsolete special-event achievements.

v1.40 — The Rare Brick Update

Random events removed. Bricks spawn every 1–5 minutes. Rainbow chance is 1 in 10,000, upgradeable to 1 in 2,000 through ten expensive Golden Shard upgrades. Rainbow Bricks originally awarded 15 shards (changed to 1 in v1.42). Golden Bricks can call a 1-in-500 reinforcement after collection. Ashen Crown now needs 10 Rainbow Bricks; Inferno Prism needs two of every Golden rarity and two Rainbow Bricks in a single Burn cycle. New achievements added.

v1.39 — Ascension Price Scaling Rebalance

Each ascension milestone now increases the next price by a growing multiplier: 1.25× initially, plus 0.10× for each additional ascension.

Achievement Name Refresh

Numbered achievement sequels now have unique, theme-matched names. Existing unlocks and progress are unchanged.

Ascension Progress & Achievement Popups

Ascensions now use cumulative studs earned across runs, with a rolling milestone bar and +N ready indicator. Achievement popups have been refreshed and can be dismissed.

Stud Skin Gallery Update

Stud Skins now has its own navigation button. Every skin shows its unlock requirements, including Complete Burning for challenge skins.

One Burn, One Legend

Burnt LEGO Challenges now award only one skin per Burn All Studs. If several are ready, choose one in a selection menu; all unchosen challenge progress resets for the next cycle.

Golden Brick Timing & Perfect Storm

Golden and Rainbow Brick appearances now occur every 1–15 minutes, with the next countdown beginning at spawn. New Golden Shard upgrades extend timed effects and brick visibility. Bricks fade in and out. Cinder Core now needs two overlapping timed effects at a 5.00× combo.

Extreme Burnt LEGO Trials

Five new multi-system feats: a no-click research empire, a perfect four-way storm, the complete secret archive, triple prismatic eclipses, and the impossible upgrade blueprint. Earn exclusive permanent skins when burning.

Burnt LEGO Challenges & Legacy Preservation

Five extreme challenge goals; Burn All Studs now preserves unlocked achievements, lifetime statistics and collected skins while resetting active progression.

Achievement Mastery & Interface Refresh

Ten achievement milestone skins, five permanent Burn Challenge skins, a live rarity breakdown, selectable click effects and a cleaner responsive interface.

500 Achievements — Unique Names

50 new individually named achievements for stud milestones, combos, Golden and Rainbow Bricks, secrets, research, ascensions and offline earnings.

Secret Hunt: Moving Marks & Corner Trail

Randomised hidden-brick placements after a full reset, fixed-location secrets, a five-corner Sitting Stud tile puzzle, and +1% production for every five hidden bricks collected.

Previous Achievement Expansion — 450 Total

150 new achievements across 15 progression series, including combo mastery, single-run challenges, secret brick discoveries, Rainbow Bricks, offline earnings, ascensions and crossover objectives. New achievements award rarity-weighted bonus points.

Combo Rebalance

Click combos now increase by 0.005× every five clicks, up to 5.00×. The combo resets after two seconds without clicking. Combo mastery achievements now require the new maximum.

Ascension Batches & Secret Challenges

Ascensions now stack and award one point per affordable tier. Twelve secret bricks require puzzles or timed challenges. Less cluttered help text and harder-to-see hidden marks.

Earlier Full Reset Update

Earlier versions used Burn All Studs as a total wipe. Current versions use Burn All Studs for a progress reset; use Full Wipe Everything in Settings if you want a completely fresh start.

Hidden Hunt & Interface Refresh

Harder-to-spot secret marks, a disguised colour puzzle, non-sticky navigation, grouped upgrade pages and a compact achievement list.

Achievement Rarities & Super Stud

Side-screen unlock notifications, category and rarity filters, 50 Super Stud challenges, and weighted rarity points toward permanent achievement bonuses.

300 Achievements Milestone

Secret Brick Hunt & Achievement Expansion

155 new achievements (300 total), including special challenges and 50 unique hidden bricks across the game. The deepest brick is at the bottom of Settings. Hidden brick discoveries are also erased by Burn All Studs.

Offline & News Update

Offline earnings, an upgrade tree, a welcome-back reward popup, a News tab, and clearer single-run achievements.
